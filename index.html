About us · เกี่ยวกับเรา

A sweet stop at the top of Central.

You'll find our gelato counter in the middle of the 4th floor at Central Plaza Udon Thani, near S&P. Our gelato is Italian-style: thick and smooth with a satisfying chew, and we're always creating new flavours.

Not sure what to pick? Just ask for a taste. Behind the counter there are comfy seats, perfect for a catch-up with friends after shopping or a meal.

  • Lots of flavours, including Thai fruits in season
  • Scoops in cups & fun ice cream sticks
  • Orders for events & merit-making: just call us
